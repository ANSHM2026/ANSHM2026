Keynote speaker

Prof. Tommy Chan

Queensland University of Technology (QUT)
Brisbane, Queensland

Biography

Prof Tommy Chan is a Full Professor of Civil Engineering in the School of Civil and Environmental Engineering at Queensland University of Technology (QUT) and a member of ARC College of Experts. He is also the Founding Chair and President of the Australian Network of Structural Health Monitoring (ANSHM). Prof Chan has been actively involved in carrying out research on structural health monitoring (SHM). He has more than 30 years of experience in SHM of various significant long-span bridges in Hong Kong and Mainland China and has taken an active role in promoting SHM in Australia after he moved to Australia in 2007, leading various significant achievements in SHM with other SHM experts, asset owners, government and industry practitioners under ANSHM. He is also a professional engineer and Fellow of IEAust and HKIE. Besides SHM, his research interests include bridge engineering, structural dynamics, impact studies, Moving Force Identification, Weigh-in-Motion Studies, optical fibre and other sensor developments, and new engineering materials.

Presentation title
Recent Advances of Structural Health Monitoring in Australia
Keynote speaker

Dr. Yew-Chin Koay

VIDA Roads
Melbourne, Victoria

Biography

Dr. Yew-Chin Koay is the Senior Specialist in Materials and Structural Engineering at the Victorian Infrastructure Delivery Authority (VIDA) Roads. With over 25 years of experience across government, industry, and academia, he has delivered innovative engineering solutions for major transport infrastructure projects throughout Victoria. Dr Koay holds a PhD from RMIT University, and he actively collaborates with universities and industry partners to bridge the gap between research and practice. He is also a strong advocate for overseas qualified engineers, supporting their professional development and integration into the Australian engineering profession. In recognition of his outstanding contributions to the engineering profession, he was awarded the 2025 Engineers Australia Victorian Professional Engineer of the Year.

Presentation title
Structural Health Monitoring: An Overview from Asset Inspection to Digital Intelligence

Keynote speaker

Prof. Brian Uy

University of New South Wales (UNSW)
Sydney, Australia

Biography

Brian Uy is the 2026 President of the Institution of Structural Engineers and Scientia Professor of Structural Engineering in the School of Civil and Environmental Engineering at the University of New South Wales, Sydney, Australia. He currently Chairs the Australian/New Zealand Standards on Steel and Composite Structures for Buildings and Bridges, BD-032 and BD-090-06 and he is a Vice President of the International Association of Bridge and Structural Engineering. Brian is an elected Fellow of the Australian Academy of Technological Sciences and Engineering, Engineers Australia, Institution of Structural Engineers, Institution of Civil Engineers, American Society of Civil Engineers, Structural Engineering Institute and the International Association of Bridge and Structural Engineers.

Presentation title
The Role of Structural Health Monitoring in the Achievement of Structural Efficiency and Reduction of Embodied Carbon
Keynote speaker

Prof. Yong Xia

The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU)
Hong Kong

Biography

Dr. Yong Xia is a Chair Professor of Structural Health Monitoring at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Associate Dean of Graduate School, and the Director of the Guangdong-Hong Kong Joint Laboratory for Marine Infrastructure. His main research areas include structural health monitoring, substructuring methods, and structural dynamics. He has completed more than 20 structural health monitoring (SHM) projects, including the 600-m tall Canton Tower, 632-m tall Shanghai Tower, the 1377-m long main span Tsing Ma Bridge and 55-km long Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macao Bridge. He has published over 200 refereed international journal papers, three research books, and more than 20 design standards. Dr. Xia is now the Co-Editor-in-Chief of Advances in Structural Engineering. He was awarded The Nishino Prize, State Technological Innovation Award, ASCE Greater China Distinguished Leadership Medal, and SHM Person of the Year Award, among many others.

Presentation title
Long-span Bridge Health Monitoring in Hong Kong
