Keynote speaker

Prof. Tommy Chan

Queensland University of Technology (QUT)
Brisbane, Queensland

Biography

Prof. Tommy Chan is a Full Professor of Civil Engineering in the School of Civil and Environmental Engineering at Queensland University of Technology (QUT) and a member of ARC College of Experts. He is also the Founding Chair and President of the Australian Network of Structural Health Monitoring (ANSHM). Prof. Chan has been actively involved in carrying out research on structural health monitoring (SHM). He has more than 30 years of experience in SHM of various significant long-span bridges in Hong Kong and Mainland China and has taken an active role in promoting SHM in Australia after he moved to Australia in 2007, leading various significant achievements in SHM with other SHM experts, asset owners, government and industry practitioners under ANSHM. He is also a professional engineer and Fellow of IEAust and HKIE. Besides SHM, his research interests include bridge engineering, structural dynamics, impact studies, Moving Force Identification, Weigh-in-Motion Studies, optical fibre and other sensor developments, and new engineering materials.

Presentation title
Recent Advances of Structural Health Monitoring in Australia
PC Speaker photo
Keynote speaker

Dr. Phil Cutter

Rockfield Technologies Australia
Perth, Western Australia

Biography

Dr. Phil Cutter is a Principal Engineer at Rockfield Technologies Australia, specialising in structural health monitoring, geotechnical instrumentation, and remote monitoring systems for civil, mining, transport, and energy infrastructure. He has extensive international experience delivering monitoring solutions for major projects including the Forrestfield-Airport Link in Perth, the Queensferry Crossing in Scotland, the Dubai and Singapore Metro networks, floating offshore structures, and offshore wind turbine developments. Phil currently leads the development and deployment of advanced monitoring technologies across Australia and internationally, with a particular focus on wireless telemetry, sensor integration, and data-driven asset management. His professional interests include the practical application of structural health monitoring technologies, remote sensing systems, digital transformation of infrastructure monitoring, and the use of monitoring data to improve the performance, safety, and resilience of critical assets.

YX Speaker photo
Keynote speaker

Prof. Yong Xia

The Hong Kong Polytechnic University
Hong Kong

Biography

Prof. Yong Xia is a Chair Professor of Structural Health Monitoring at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Associate Dean of Graduate School, and the Director of the Guangdong-Hong Kong Joint Laboratory for Marine Infrastructure. His main research areas include structural health monitoring, substructuring methods, and structural dynamics. He has completed more than 20 structural health monitoring (SHM) projects, including the 600-m tall Canton Tower, 632-m tall Shanghai Tower, the 1377-m long main span Tsing Ma Bridge and 55-km long Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macao Bridge. He has published over 200 refereed international journal papers, three research books, and more than 20 design standards. Prof. Xia is now the Co-Editor-in-Chief of Advances in Structural Engineering. He was awarded The Nishino Prize, State Technological Innovation Award, ASCE Greater China Distinguished Leadership Medal, and SHM Person of the Year Award, among many others.

Presentation title
